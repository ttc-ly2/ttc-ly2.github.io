Fringe Separation Calculation

Formula: y = λD / a

λ = 500 nm = 500 x 10-9 m

D = 3 m

a = 0.5 mm = 0.5 x 10-3 m
y = (500x10-9 * 3) / 0.5x10-3

y = 0.003 m
y = 3.00 mm
